## Car valeting in Shrewsbury, done on your drive

Shrewsbury is one of our regular runs from Telford. It’s a straight shot along the A5, and a lot of our Shrewsbury customers know that road better than they’d like, because they commute along it every day. That’s usually how the conversation starts: “The car’s always filthy, and I never have time to sort it.”

That’s the whole point of a mobile valet. You don’t queue at a car wash on the ring road, you don’t sit in a waiting room, and you don’t lose your Saturday. We turn up at your house in Meole Brace or Bayston Hill, set up on the drive, and get on with it. You put the kettle on, or carry on working from the spare room.

## What Shrewsbury does to a car

Every town is hard on cars in its own way. Here’s what we see most on Shrewsbury cars:

- **A5 and A49 road film.** Spray from the dual carriageway leaves a greasy grey haze on the lower half of the car and the back window. A proper wash with tar removal gets it off; a car wash brush just smears it round.
- **Town-centre parking.** Tight spaces in the old streets around the castle and the Square mean door dings and scuffs on bumpers. We can’t fix dents, but a polish can make light scuffs far less obvious, and paint correction deals with the swirls left by years of rushed washing.
- **The river.** The Severn wraps right round the middle of Shrewsbury. Damp mornings and low cloud mean condensation, and the inside of the windows on an older car can get a film you only notice when the sun’s low. A full valet cleans the glass inside and out.
- **Trees and parks.** Leafy roads in Copthorne and Belle Vue are lovely to live on and terrible for paintwork. Sap and bird droppings are acidic and etch into the clear coat if they’re left. The sooner they’re cleaned off, the less damage they do.
