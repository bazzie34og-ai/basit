## Mobile valeting in Newport

Newport is a proper market town: a wide High Street, independent shops, and fields on every side. Head out in any direction and within a couple of minutes you’re on a farm road. That’s lovely for a Sunday walk. It’s less lovely for your car.

We’re a mobile valeting service based in Telford, about fifteen minutes away up the A518. We come to your home, your farm or your workplace in Newport and the villages around it, and we clean the car where it’s parked. You don’t need to find a car wash, and you don’t need to give up an afternoon.

## Farm traffic and what it leaves behind

Around Newport the roads are shared with tractors, trailers, muck spreaders and the occasional combine. If you live in Edgmond, Chetwynd, Tibberton or Lilleshall, you already know the result:

- **Mud and slurry on the lower panels.** It dries into a crust along the sills and up the back of the car. Scrubbing it off dry scratches the paint, so we soak it and rinse it before anything touches the surface.
- **Muck in the arches.** Packed mud in the wheel arches holds moisture against the metal. It’s worth getting out properly a few times a year, especially in winter when there’s salt on the A41 and A518 as well.
- **Footwells full of field.** Wellies, dogs and kids after a muddy walk all bring the outside in. A [full valet & deep clean](/full-valet-telford/#deep-clean) gets the carpets and mats back to something you’d be happy to sit on.

Pickups and 4x4s are a big part of what we clean out here. A Ford Ranger owner wrote that Chris “made my ranger look brand new”, and on the [monthly plan](/monthly-valet-plan/) a 4x4 or SUV is £60 a visit.

## Winter on the A41 and A518

The main roads in and out of Newport get gritted hard every winter, and the farm lanes don’t get gritted at all. That means salt on one side and mud on the other. The salt is the bigger problem: it clings to the sills, the wheel arches and the underside, and it keeps working long after the snow has gone. A full valet at the end of winter rinses it out of the places you can’t see, and the monthly plan stops it building up in the first place.
