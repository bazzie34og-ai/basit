## Valeting where the Iron Bridge stands

Our whole look comes from this place. The Iron Bridge was cast in 1779, the first of its kind in the world, and it still spans the Severn at the bottom of the Gorge. It’s the reason the arch is drawn across this website. So it feels right that Ironbridge, Madeley and Broseley are some of our favourite places to work.

They’re also some of the hardest on a car. Steep, narrow roads, trees everywhere, a river in the bottom of the valley and a lot of birds. We’re a mobile valeting service from Telford, a few minutes up the hill, and we come to your driveway to deal with all of it.

## The Gorge: lovely place, tough on paint

- **Tree sap.** The wooded slopes of the Gorge, Lincoln Hill and Benthall Edge drip sap all summer. It lands as tiny sticky dots that set hard in the sun and slowly etch into the clear coat. Washing alone won’t shift it once it’s set; it needs softening and lifting.
- **Bird lime.** Plenty of birds in those trees, and what comes out of them is acidic. Left for a week on a hot bonnet it leaves a dull crater-shaped mark. If yours already has a few, [paint correction](/paint-correction-telford/) from £160 can usually improve them a great deal, though deep etching can’t always be removed completely.
- **River damp.** The Severn runs right through the bottom of the Gorge. Cars parked along the Wharfage or in Jackfield stay damp longer, and the inside steams up. A [full valet](/full-valet-telford/) cleans the glass properly inside and out and freshens the cabin.
- **Steep hills and brake dust.** Going up and down Madeley Road, the Lloyds and Broseley’s hills is hard work for brakes. Brake dust bakes onto alloy wheels and pits them over time.

## Heritage town, visitor traffic

The Gorge is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and busy with visitors most of the year. If you live or work here you know what that means: narrow roads, tight parking, and a car that gets brushed past by everybody. Light scuffs and swirls build up. A [mini valet & polish](/mini-valet-telford/) from £45 makes a surprising difference, and detailing brings back the depth.

If you run a guest house, café or holiday let in Ironbridge and want your own car or van looking smart for guests, ask about regular visits. See [van valeting](/van-valeting-telford/) for work vehicles.

## Madeley and Broseley

**Madeley** is busy family territory: school runs, sports kits, dogs, and car seats with years of crumbs in them. That’s exactly what the full valet & deep clean (from £90) is for, and the [monthly plan](/monthly-valet-plan/) keeps it from getting that bad again.

**Broseley** sits on the other side of the river, with its old pipeworks and steep lanes. Lots of older houses, lots of on-street parking under trees, and lots of cars that need sap taking off every few weeks in summer.

## The monthly plan makes the most sense here

Sap and bird droppings do the most harm when they’re left for weeks. Our monthly plan visits once a month, cleans everything off before it can etch, and adds hydrophobic paint protection so the next lot doesn’t bond as hard. It’s £30 a visit for a small car, up to £60 for a 4x4 or SUV. For longer-lasting protection, ask about [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating-telford/).
