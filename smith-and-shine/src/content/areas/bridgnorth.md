## Valeting in a town built on a cliff

Bridgnorth must be the only town in Shropshire where you need to know which half you live in. High Town sits on the sandstone ridge; Low Town sits down by the Severn; and the Cliff Railway, the Cartway and a lot of steep steps join the two together. It’s a beautiful place. It’s also a hard place to keep a car clean.

We’re a mobile valeting service from Telford, about twenty-five minutes down the A442. We come to your home in Bridgnorth or one of the villages round it, set up on the drive or outside the house, and clean the car where it is. No queue, no hunting for a car wash, no driving back up the hill in the rain.

## What the Severn valley does to your car

- **Damp that never quite goes.** The river keeps Low Town misty on autumn mornings, and cars parked near the water get a fine film of moisture on everything. That damp gets inside too: steamy windows, a musty smell, and green mould in the window seals of older cars if they’re left long enough. A [full valet](/full-valet-telford/) cleans the glass inside and out and leaves the cabin fresh.
- **Hills and brakes.** Every trip up and down from High Town works your brakes hard, and brake dust is one of the worst things for alloy wheels. It’s hot, sharp and slightly corrosive, and it bakes onto the wheel face. Our monthly plan includes alloy wheels cleaned and protected every visit, so it never builds up.
- **Trees, birds and the riverside.** Cars parked under trees along the river or near Severn Park pick up sap and bird droppings. Both are acidic and both etch the clear coat if they’re left in the sun. If yours already has dull, etched marks, [paint correction](/paint-correction-telford/) can usually improve them.
- **Rural lanes.** Out towards Worfield, Claverley and Morville, the lanes bring mud and gravel. Wheel arches fill up, sills get sandblasted, and stone chips appear on the bonnet.

## The right clean for Bridgnorth

The [monthly plan](/monthly-valet-plan/) suits Bridgnorth better than almost anywhere. Damp, brake dust and tree sap all do the most harm when they’re left for weeks, and a monthly 12-point clean, with hydrophobic paint protection, stops them settling in. It’s £30 a visit for a small car, £40 for medium, £50 for large and £60 for a 4x4 or SUV.

If it’s been a while, start with a [full valet](/full-valet-telford/) from £70, or the deep clean from £90 if the inside smells of wet dog and riverside walks.

## Classics and the Severn Valley crowd

Bridgnorth is the northern end of the Severn Valley Railway, and the town attracts people who like old machinery looked after properly. That goes for cars too. We see classics, weekend cars and pride-and-joys all over this area. For those, [car detailing](/car-detailing-telford/) from £110 is the place to start, and a [ceramic coating](/ceramic-coating-telford/) helps keep that finish through a damp Shropshire winter.

## No drive? Not a problem

Plenty of houses in Low Town and the older streets of High Town don’t have a drive at all. If your car lives on the street, we can often still clean it there, as long as it’s safe, we’re not blocking anyone, and there’s room to work round it. Tell us where the car will be when you book, and send a photo of the spot if it’s tight. Chris will tell you honestly if it’ll work, and if it won’t, we’ll find another way, such as doing it while you’re at work.
