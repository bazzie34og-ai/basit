## Car valeting in Shifnal: we’re practically neighbours

If you follow us on social media you’ll have noticed a lot of our recent posts are tagged Shifnal. That’s no accident. It’s just along the M54 from Telford, it’s full of people who like their cars looked after, and it’s full of country lanes that make sure those cars never stay clean for long.

Albrighton is the same story a few miles further east. Lovely village, quiet lanes, and a car that looks like it’s done a rally stage by Wednesday.

We’re a mobile valeting service, so we come to you. Driveway, farmyard, the space outside your unit: if we can get round the car, we can clean it. You don’t need to find a car wash, and you definitely don’t need to drive your clean car back down a muddy lane afterwards.

## What the lanes and the M54 do to your car

- **Mud, all year round.** The lanes between Shifnal, Kemberton, Ryton and Albrighton carry tractors and trailers in every season. Mud ends up packed in the wheel arches, splattered up the doors and walked into the footwells. Left alone, the grit in it scratches the paint every time you brush past.
- **Commuter grime.** Junctions 3 and 4 of the M54 put Shifnal and Albrighton within easy reach of Telford and Wolverhampton. Every motorway mile adds a film of traffic grime and, in winter, road salt. Salt is the one that really matters: it sits in the arches and on the sills and gets to work on anything metal.
- **Wet verges and gravel drives.** Pulling onto a soft verge to let a tractor past is very Shropshire. So is the stone chip that follows. Our [paint protection film](/paint-protection-film-telford/) page explains how to stop those chips reaching the paint.

Shifnal has grown a lot, and the new estates on the edge of town are full of nearly-new cars on fresh block paving.
