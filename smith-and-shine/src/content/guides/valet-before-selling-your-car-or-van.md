Whether you’re selling privately, part-exchanging or handing back a lease, the state of the car makes a difference to how quickly it goes and what you get for it. Buyers make up their minds fast, and most of that decision is about how the car looks and feels before they’ve even turned the key.

A valet is one of the cheapest ways to make a good impression.

## Why clean cars sell better

**They get more clicks.** Most cars are sold online now. Your photos are competing with dozens of others on the same screen. A shiny car with a clean interior stands out; a dull one with a cluttered back seat gets scrolled past.

**They feel looked after.** Buyers can’t see the service history from a photo, but they can see a stained seat and a dashboard full of dust. A clean car suggests a careful owner. A dirty one makes people wonder what else has been ignored.

**They give buyers less to haggle with.** Every mark, stain and smell is a reason to knock money off. Take those reasons away and you’re negotiating from a much better place.

## What buyers notice first

- **The driver’s seat.** Wear, stains and the bolster closest to the door.
- **The steering wheel and gear knob.** Grime here makes a car feel high-mileage.
- **The smell.** Smoke, dogs and damp put people off instantly.
- **The footwells and boot.** Mud and clutter suggest a hard life.
- **The windows.** A hazy windscreen looks neglected.
- **The paint in sunlight.** Swirls and dullness make a car look older than it is.
- **The wheels.** Brake dust-caked alloys spoil the whole side profile.

A proper [full valet](/full-valet-telford/) deals with almost all of that in one visit.

## Getting the photos right

Once it’s clean, don’t waste it on bad photos.

1. **Timing.** Take the photos the same day or the day after the valet, before the car gets used.
2. **Light.** Bright overcast days are best: even light, no harsh reflections. Avoid midday sun.
3. **Background.** A plain wall or open space. Not the bins.
4. **Angles.** Front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, both sides, the dashboard, the front seats, the back seats, the boot and the wheels.
5. **Declutter.** Take everything out. Phone holders, air fresheners, the lot.
6. **Show the good bits.** Clean alloys, tidy seats, a gleaming bonnet. You’ve paid for them; make sure buyers see them.

## Lease returns

Handing back a lease car? A proper clean helps the inspection go smoothly and shows the car has been cared for. If there are swirls or light scratches on the paint, ask about [paint correction](/paint-correction-telford/) first. A clean car won’t hide genuine damage, but it means the inspector is looking at the car, not the dirt.

## A quick pre-sale checklist

Before the valet:

- Empty the glovebox, door pockets, boot and under the seats.
- Find both keys, the service book and the V5C.
- Remove dashcams, phone holders and stickers you don’t want in the photos.

After the valet:

- Take the photos within a day or two.
- Keep the car out of the rain if you can, or at least away from trees.
- Pop a cloth and a bottle of quick detailer in the boot for the day of the viewing.
- Open the windows for ten minutes before buyers arrive.
