“How often should I get it done?” comes up on nearly every first visit, usually as the customer is looking at their freshly cleaned car and wondering how to keep it like that.

The honest answer depends on how you use the car. But for most people around Telford and Shropshire, there’s a sweet spot, and it’s monthly. Here’s why.

## What happens when you leave it

A car doesn’t just get dirty. Some of what lands on it is actively harmful, and the longer it stays there the more damage it does.

- **Bird droppings** are acidic. On a warm day they can etch a dull mark into the clear coat within days. You’ve probably seen the ghostly outline left behind after one has been washed off too late.
- **Tree sap** sets hard and bonds to the paint. If you park under trees, which in places like Ironbridge, Madeley and Wellington is hard to avoid, it builds up fast.
- **Road salt** in winter sits in the wheel arches, on the sills and underneath. It’s there to melt ice, and it’s very good at encouraging rust.
- **Brake dust** is hot, sharp metal particles. It bakes onto alloy wheels and slowly pits them. Hilly towns like Bridgnorth make it worse.
- **Traffic film** from the M54 and A5 leaves a greasy haze that dulls the paint and smears across the windscreen.

Inside, it’s a different kind of damage. Grit in the carpets acts like sandpaper every time you put your feet down. Spills soak into foam. Crumbs attract more crumbs. The longer it’s left, the harder and more expensive it is to put right.

## The rough guide

Here’s how we’d think about it.

**Every week:** Only really needed for show cars, or if you’re genuinely obsessive. (No judgement. We get it.)

**Every two to four weeks:** Ideal for cars that work hard: long commutes, farm lanes, dog walks, school runs, work vans. Some of our regulars are on a two-week rhythm. Chris’s reply to one Ranger owner was simply “See you in 2 weeks buddy.”

**Every month:** The sweet spot for most people. Often enough that nothing gets the chance to do damage, and the car always looks cared for.

**Every three to six months:** Fine for a car that does low miles and lives in a garage. You’ll want a proper full valet each time, because things will have built up.

**Once a year, or when it gets embarrassing:** We see a lot of these, and we’re always happy to. But you’ll be paying for a rescue each time rather than a clean, and some of the damage (etched paint, worn carpets, pitted wheels) won’t fully come back.

## Why monthly works so well

When a car is cleaned every month, three things happen.

**Nothing gets the chance to bond.** Sap, droppings and salt get removed before they cause harm. The paint stays healthy, so it stays glossy.

**Each clean is quicker.** A car that was done four weeks ago doesn’t need scraping out. That’s why our monthly plan costs less per visit than a one-off valet: less time, so a lower price.

**Protection gets topped up.** Our monthly visit includes hydrophobic paint protection every time. It makes water bead and slide off, which means rain actually helps keep the car cleaner between visits.

## Do it yourself in between?

You can, and some regulars do. A few tips if you do:

- Use two buckets: one with soapy water, one with clean water to rinse the mitt.
- Never use the same sponge on the wheels and the paint.
- Avoid automatic car washes with brushes. They’re the main cause of swirls.
- Get bird droppings off quickly, with plenty of water and a soft cloth.
