## 10. Service and suburb page specs (7.5 and 7.6)

Every service page renders its spec's H1, title, must-include sections (as H2s in `src/content/services/*.ts`), the 5 spec FAQs (plus 1–2 from the bank), related services, the 20-suburb grid, a link to `/` with the anchor "bond cleaning Brisbane", and a quote form at the top and bottom.

Every suburb page renders the local facts, access notes, what agents check, a unique "[Suburb] specifics" section, 2 photos, 5–6 service links, 5 FAQs (the 2 local ones from the spec plus 3 reworded price / timing / do-I-need-to-be-home questions), nearby suburbs from the brief, and the homepage link. The real-job block is built and hidden.

`npm run audit` checks all of this on the built HTML: 0 problems at hand-over.
