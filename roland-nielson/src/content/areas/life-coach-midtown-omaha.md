## Close to everything, still running behind

Midtown puts a lot within reach. Work, dinner, the kids' school and a park can all sit inside a couple of miles. You'd think that would make life easier.

What men tell me is the opposite. When everything is close, everything gets squeezed in. There's no drive to think, no gap between one role and the next. You walk straight from a hard meeting into a bath-time meltdown and wonder why you snapped.

So we build in a pause. It might be ten minutes in the car before you go inside. It might be a short list on Sunday night so Monday doesn't ambush you. Small things, done on purpose, make tomorrow easier. That's most of what we do together.
