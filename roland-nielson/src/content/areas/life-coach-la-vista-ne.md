I'm a service-area coach, so I don't have an office in La Vista or anywhere else. We set up each meeting by appointment.

If you want to move, we walk. Central Park is handy, and Walnut Creek is a few minutes away when you want a longer loop. If you'd rather sit, we meet at your place when it's private or in a meeting space I book for the session.

Video and phone are the backup for weeks that go sideways. A late shift, the kids staying over, a car in the shop. We still meet, just not in person.

## When you're starting over close to everything

La Vista sits right in the middle of things. That makes it a common place to land when life changes fast: a divorce, a job move, getting sober, coming back after a rough stretch.

Starting over isn't one big decision. It's a hundred small ones in the first few months. When to get up. What to do with a Friday night alone. How to talk to your ex without it turning into a fight. Whether the kids' room looks like a room or a storage closet.

We take those one at a time. You walk away from each session with two or three things to do before we talk again, and I check in on them between sessions. Over a few months, the place you landed starts to feel like a place you chose.
