## Privacy in a town where everybody knows everybody

Ralston is surrounded by Omaha on most sides, but it still feels like its own small town. That's a good thing most days. It's harder on the day you want help.

I get it. Where I grew up, people talked too. So I keep this simple. I don't share names. I don't post about who I coach. I won't wave at you in the store unless you wave first.

That privacy matters because the work only happens when you're honest. If you're worried about how something sounds, you'll leave it out, and then we're working on the wrong problem. In here, you can say the real thing. We start there and build a plan around it.
