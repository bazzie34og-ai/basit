## Coaching for a week that isn't 9-to-5

Most self-help is written for a man with a day job who gets home for dinner. That's not Benson for a lot of guys.

So we build your plan around the week you actually live. If your "morning" starts at noon, that's when your routine starts. If Friday and Saturday are your hardest shifts, we plan for the drive home on those nights, not some generic Monday.

The three ideas still hold. Make tomorrow easier with one small thing done tonight. Stop before you react when you walk in tired and the house is loud. Do what you said, even when what you said was small. Those don't care what time you clock out.
