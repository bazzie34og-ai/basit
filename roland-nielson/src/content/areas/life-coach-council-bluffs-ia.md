I don't keep an office in Council Bluffs or in Omaha. We meet where it works for you, by appointment.

For men on the Iowa side, that often means a walk. Lake Manawa has room to move and talk without anybody close by. If you work downtown, Bayliss Park is a quick option. Some men like walking the Bob Kerrey bridge, half in Iowa and half in Nebraska, while we go over the week.

If you'd rather sit, we can use a private meeting space or a quiet room at your place. And when shifts or traffic get in the way, phone and video keep us on schedule. Lots of men mix both.
