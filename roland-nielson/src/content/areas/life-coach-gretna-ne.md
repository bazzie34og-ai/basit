## When the commute eats your evening

The drive isn't just time. It's what happens to your head during it.

You leave work still carrying the day. You sit in traffic and replay it. Then you walk in the door and your wife asks a simple question, and you snap. Nobody did anything wrong. You just didn't have a gap between work and home.

So we build one. Maybe it's five minutes in the driveway before you go inside. Maybe it's a different podcast, or no podcast, or a call to a friend on the way. We test it for a week and adjust. Small change, but it's often the difference between a good night at home and another one you regret.
