## Privacy in a small town

In a place where folks know your truck by sight, privacy matters. I get it. A man doesn't want to be the guy who "needs help" at the next ball game.

So let me be plain. I don't share who I work with. I don't need you to explain yourself to anybody. And coaching is training, not fixing. You're not broken. You're a capable man who wants to stop before you react, keep your word and build a better week.

If a wife, mother or brother is the one looking, [families can reach out first](/for-families/), and I'll take it from there quietly.
