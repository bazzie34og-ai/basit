I don't have an office in Bellevue or anywhere else. We meet by appointment wherever makes sense for you.

Some men want to walk. Fontenelle Forest is good for that: shade and no one close enough to listen. Others want a table and a door that shuts, so we sit down at your place or in a private meeting space I set up for the session. Sessions happen evenings and Saturdays, and you pick what fits on the booking page.

If you're on a schedule that changes every month, don't fight it. Video and phone sessions keep you steady when you can't predict next week. Lots of Bellevue men use both.

## Coaching that moves when you move

Most coaching assumes you'll be in the same place for a year. A lot of families here can't count on that.

So I build the work to travel. Your plan lives on paper, not in my office. The check-ins happen by text and video, so they keep going if you relocate. If you leave the area, we keep working online with the same plan. If you're leaving the service, we work on the hard part nobody briefs you on: building your own structure when nobody hands you a schedule anymore.

What I care about is simple: you come home and you're actually there.
