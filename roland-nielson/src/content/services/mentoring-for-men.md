I didn't have a man to show me how. My dad wasn't around. My mom was fighting her own battles. The older guys in my neighborhood were teaching lessons I'm still unlearning.

So I learned most things the slow way. Wrong first, then a little less wrong. How to hold a job. How to apologize. How to stay when I wanted to leave.

## Mentoring vs coaching sessions, honestly

Here's the straight version. In a coaching session, I ask a lot of questions and you do most of the talking. The plan comes from you, because a plan you build is one you'll keep. That's still the backbone of how I work.

Mentoring for men is different. It's me saying, "Here's what happened when I tried that." It's stories, opinions and the mistakes I'd skip if I could go back. Some weeks you need that more than another question.

You don't have to pick one. You tell me what you need, and I'll tell you which hat I'm wearing so it's always clear when I'm sharing my road and when I'm helping you find yours.

## Why it matters to have someone who's been there

A lot of men carry quiet shame about not knowing things they think they should know by now. Having an older guy in your corner takes that pressure off. You can ask the dumb question. You can admit you blew it.
