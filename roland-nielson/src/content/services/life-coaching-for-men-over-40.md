Somewhere past 40, most men do the math. Half the years are probably gone. The plan you had at 25 didn't turn out the way you pictured. And the next decade is going to pass whether you steer it or not.

That can feel like a punch. It can also be the most useful thing that happens to you.

## Why the forties feel so heavy

By now you're carrying a lot. A mortgage. Kids who need rides and attention. A job you can't walk away from. A body that doesn't bounce back. Most men keep their heads down, then look up and wonder where the time went.

The weight isn't a sign you've failed. It's a sign you've been carrying everything for everyone with nobody checking on you.

## What life coaching for men over 40 changes

We stop trying to fix everything. Instead we pick the few things that would make the biggest difference over the next ten years, and we give them real time each week.

For one man that's his health, because he wants to meet his grandkids. For another it's his marriage or a job that drains him. The plan is yours. My job is to keep it from fading by spring.
