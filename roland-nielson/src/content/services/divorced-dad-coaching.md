Nobody tells you how quiet it gets. Sunday night you drop the kids off, drive back to a place that doesn't feel like home yet, and the silence hits you before you get your boots off.

A lot of dads fill that silence badly. A few beers turns into a twelve-pack. By Friday you're tired and short-tempered, and the weekend you waited all week for doesn't go the way you pictured.

## Why the weekends feel so heavy

When time with your kids gets cut down, every hour starts to feel like a test. So you overplan. You buy too much. You try to cram a week of fathering into two days, and everyone ends up worn out.

Then contact with your ex sets you off, and you say something in a text you can't unsend.

My dad wasn't around when I was growing up. I know what it does to a kid when a father drifts. I also know it doesn't take a perfect setup to stay close. It takes showing up, again and again, when you said you would.

## What divorced dad coaching changes

We start with the days you're alone, because that's where the rest of the week gets won or lost. Then we work on hand-offs, hard talks and making your place feel like home.

Divorced dad coaching isn't about getting over it fast. It's about being steady enough that your kids can lean on you while you rebuild.

## The man your kids get to watch

Kids see how their father handles a hard season. Over time, yours get to see a man who stays calm with their mom, keeps his word, and builds something new without falling apart. That lesson lasts longer than any weekend trip.
