Some men have a boss who rides them all day. Some have one living in their own head. That second one is worse, because he follows you home, gets in bed with you and is waiting when you wake up.

He says things like "You'll mess this up too," "It's too late for you," and "Who do you think you are?" Most men never question him. They just obey.

## Why your head works against you

Your mind is built to keep you safe, not happy. It remembers every failure, every embarrassment, every time someone let you down. Then it uses that to keep you from trying anything new. It thinks it's protecting you.

Add in stress, bad sleep, a phone full of other men's highlight reels and a few hard years, and the voice gets loud. It starts sounding like the truth.

I had that voice for a long time. Growing up in North Omaha, with my mom struggling and my dad gone, I had plenty of evidence that things don't work out. It took me years to learn I didn't have to believe everything I thought.

## What mindset coaching for men looks like here

We don't sit around repeating affirmations. We catch the thoughts, look at them in daylight, and test them. Then we take one action that pushes back. Over weeks, the voice gets quieter because your life keeps proving it wrong.

## Life with a quieter head

You still have hard days. But the spiral is shorter. You try things you used to talk yourself out of. You sleep better. And when the old voice shows up, you recognize it, and you know you don't have to take orders from it.
