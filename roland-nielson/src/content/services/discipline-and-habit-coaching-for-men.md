Most men I talk to aren't confused. They know they should sleep more, drink less, move their body and put the phone down at night. Ask them and they'll tell you the whole plan.

The gap isn't in their head. It's between the head and the hands. And no amount of reading about habits closes it.

## Why you keep skipping it

Here's what I've seen, in my own life and in the men I coach. We pick habits that are too big for a tired man. We count on motivation, which shows up for about three days. And we leave every temptation within arm's reach, then blame ourselves for grabbing it.

One missed day turns into a lost month. That's not a character flaw. It's bad design.

I didn't rebuild my life with some big burst of willpower. There was no single moment. I kept showing up for small things until they were just what I did. That's the method I teach, because it's the one that worked when nothing else did.

## What discipline really looks like

Discipline isn't grinding harder. It's making the right thing the easy thing, and doing it on days you don't feel like it.

In discipline and habit coaching we work on one thing at a time. We cut it down, set up your house and your schedule to help, and keep score honestly. When it holds for a few weeks, we stack the next one on top.

## What changes

You stop arguing with yourself every morning, because the decision was made the night before. You quit waiting to feel ready. Slowly, you start to trust your own word again.

Your family notices. You notice most, because you're finally doing what you said you'd do.
