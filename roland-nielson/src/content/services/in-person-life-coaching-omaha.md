Some men can talk on a screen all day. Others go flat the moment the camera turns on. They answer short, check the other tab and hang up feeling like nothing happened.

If that's you, nothing is wrong with you. You just do better in the same room, and that's what in-person life coaching is for.

## How in-person sessions work without an office

I don't rent an office, and I don't plan to. Instead, we meet where you'll be most honest. That might be a private meeting space I book for our appointment, your own place once the house is quiet, or outside on a trail.

Each in-person session adds $40 to your plan to cover the room and the drive. Video and phone are always included, so you can switch whenever the week gets tight.

## Walk-and-talk coaching in Omaha

A lot of men think better on their feet. Side by side, with something to look at besides each other, the hard stuff comes out easier. These are the spots I use most:

- **Keystone Trail**, a long, flat path through the middle of the city with plenty of quiet stretches.
- **Zorinsky Lake**, a loop around the water on the west side where we can walk without being overheard.
- **Standing Bear Lake**, an easy walk up in northwest Omaha that stays calm most evenings.
- **Gene Leahy Mall at The RiverFront**, downtown, handy if you want to meet near the office.

We keep moving, keep our voices low and adjust for the weather. See how [coaching across Omaha](/omaha-life-coach/) works, then set up [a free 20-minute call](/free-call/) to pick your spot.
