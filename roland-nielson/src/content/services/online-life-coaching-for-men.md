Most men don't quit coaching because they stop caring. They quit because the drive across town on a weeknight is one more thing on a full plate. After a long shift, getting back in the car is the last thing anyone wants.

Online life coaching takes that excuse off the table. You're home, or close to it. You open a link or answer a call, and forty-five minutes later you're back with your family.

## Why video and phone work for so many men

The work is the same either way. We look at your week, find where it breaks down and set a few commitments you'll report on next time. None of that needs a room.

For some men, the screen even helps. There's a little distance. You can look away when it gets hard. Some of the most honest things men say come over the phone from a dark parking lot. And because nobody sees you walk in anywhere, you can keep this as private as you like.

## Coaching from anywhere, on your schedule

You don't have to live in Omaha. I work with men by video or phone wherever they are, evenings and Saturdays. There's no extra fee for online sessions, ever.
