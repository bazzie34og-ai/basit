Most men who feel this way aren't lazy. They pay the bills and show up. But somewhere the reasons got quiet, and now the days just happen to them.

I know that feeling. For years I didn't have a direction. I had survival. When things changed for me in 2011, it wasn't a big calling. I picked something small to work toward and kept showing up for it.

## Why purpose fades in your thirties and forties

You spend your twenties reacting. A job comes up, you take it. A kid comes along, you work more. Ten years later you're good at a life you never actually chose.

Purpose doesn't come back from thinking harder about it on the couch. It comes back when you start doing things that line up with what you care about, and you notice the difference. That's why purpose and direction coaching is mostly about action, not big talks.

## How we find direction without blowing up your life

We don't start by quitting anything. We start by looking at where your week goes and what you'd want your kids to say about you. From there we write one plain sentence about the next three years.

Then we test it. If you think you'd like a different line of work, you talk to someone doing it. If you want to build something, you give it one evening a week. Every session, we look at what you tried and what it taught you.
