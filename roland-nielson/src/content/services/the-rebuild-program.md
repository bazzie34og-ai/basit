If you're coming out of addiction, a divorce, a lost job or years of drifting, and you're ready to put real effort in for a few months, this is built for you.

Every man's timeline is different, but most follow this shape.

## Three phases

### Stabilize (first month)

Stop the bleeding. Sleep, routine, money basics and a written plan for the hardest hours of your week. We find what keeps knocking you down and put guardrails around it.

### Rebuild (middle months)

Habits, discipline and relationships. Keeping your word to yourself and to others. Hard conversations at home. The work and goals you've been putting off. Weekly sessions, plus check-ins between them.

### Sustain (final stretch)

Make it yours. We test the plan against real life, get ready for setbacks and set long-term goals so the changes hold after the program ends.

## What a week looks like

Every session starts with the same question: did you do what you said? Then we look at what got in the way, adjust the plan and set the next few commitments. Evenings or Saturdays, in person, by video or by phone.

## Three months or six

Three months suits men with one or two big areas to rebuild and a decent foundation to stand on. Six months suits men rebuilding after addiction or a major life change, or anyone who wants time for new habits to really set.

Why that long? Most of what we're changing took years to build. A few weeks feels better. A few months makes it last without me.
