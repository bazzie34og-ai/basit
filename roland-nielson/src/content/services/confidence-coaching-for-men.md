Second-guessing feels like being careful. It isn't. It's a tax you pay on every choice, big and small, and it adds up to years of standing still.

Men tell me they want to feel confident first, and then they'll speak up, make the move, have the talk. That's backwards. The feeling comes after the action, not before.

## Where the doubt comes from

Usually from a record. You told yourself you'd quit drinking and didn't. You said you'd start the business and didn't. You trusted your gut once and it went badly. Now your own mind reminds you of all of it every time you try to choose.

It makes sense. If a friend broke his word to you that many times, you'd stop trusting him too. The fix isn't to hype yourself up. It's to start keeping your word to yourself, in small ways, until the record changes.

I know this from experience. After years of bad decisions and addiction, I didn't trust a word I said. Starting in 2011, I rebuilt that trust the slow way, one kept promise after another.

## How confidence coaching for men works

We pick small, clear promises and you keep them. We set rules so everyday decisions stop eating your energy. We name the thing you've been avoiding and prepare for it together.

Each week you come back with more proof. Not proof that you're perfect. Proof that you can decide, act and recover.

## What it looks like a few months in

You speak once in the meeting, then twice. You make the call on the house or the job and stop circling it. You say no without a five-minute excuse. And when doubt shows up, it's quieter, because you've got a growing list of times you came through.
