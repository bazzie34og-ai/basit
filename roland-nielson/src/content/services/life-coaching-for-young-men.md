I was a young man who drifted hard. I grew up in North Omaha without my dad, spent time in juvenile detention and wasted years I'd love to have back. Nobody sat me down and showed me how to build a life.

So I get it when a guy in his 20s tells me he feels behind.

## Why so many young men feel stuck

You're told to figure out your future, but nobody explains how. School ends and the structure goes with it. Your phone is designed to eat your evenings. Everyone online seems to be winning, and you're working a job you don't care about, staying up too late and wondering what's wrong with you.

Nothing's wrong with you. You just don't have a plan or anyone asking about it.

## What changes with a coach

We start small. Not "find your purpose." More like: get up at the same time three days this week. Put twenty dollars in savings. Apply to one job. Then you tell me how it went.

Those small wins stack up. After a few months you have proof that you can do what you say. That's where real confidence comes from, and it carries into everything else: work, money, friendships and relationships.

## Start now, not at 40

A lot of the men I coach are in their 40s, wishing someone had pushed them at 25. You have time on your side. Use it.
