## 1. Website link

Put this on the profile as the **Website** (replace `{SITE_URL}` with the live domain):

```
{SITE_URL}/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp
```

The UTM tags let you see in the lead emails and the CSV export which quote requests came from the Google listing.
