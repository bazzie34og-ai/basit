## What drives the cost of ceramic coating?

Whoever you get a quote from, the same four things move the number.

### 1. The size of the car

More paint means more product and more time. A small hatch has far less surface area than a LandCruiser 300 or a dual-cab ute with a canopy. That's why most businesses, us included, keep the option of pricing big vehicles differently. If you drive something large, tell us the exact model when you ask for a quote, and mention anything fitted, like a bull bar or canopy.

### 2. How many layers

A single layer of graphene ceramic gives you the core benefits: a hydrophobic surface that sheds water and dirt, easier washing and a protective layer over the clear coat. A second layer deepens the gloss and gives a more premium look. That's the difference between our {{price.single}} and {{price.double}} packages.

Do you need two layers? Not always. For a daily driver that mostly needs easier washing, one layer is often enough. We'd rather tell you that than upsell you.

### 3. Interior and wheels

Paint is only part of the car. Interior protection adds stain protection to fabric and leather, and protects vinyl against fading, UV, cracking and splitting. Alloy wheel protection makes brake dust and grime easier to wash off. Bundled packages usually work out better value than adding pieces one at a time, which is why Diamond Package Plus and Ultimate Plus include wheel protection free.

### 4. Paint preparation

This is the part that varies most between providers, and the part you can't see in a photo. A coating bonds to whatever is on the paint. If contamination is left behind, it gets sealed in.

Our prep on every car is a full wash, chemical decontamination, then a clay bar to make the paint smooth. A car with heavy contamination or other issues may need more time, which is one of the reasons a price can change, and why we'd always confirm it before starting.

One honest note: a ceramic coating isn't a scratch repair. It doesn't remove swirl marks or scratches already in the paint. If a quote seems to promise that, ask exactly what work is included.

## What should be included in a ceramic coating quote?

Whether you choose us or someone else, a good quote should answer these questions clearly:

- **What product is being applied?** Brand and product name, not just "ceramic".
- **Who applies it?** A trained applicator, or whoever is free that day?
- **What prep is included?** Wash, decontamination, clay, and anything else.
- **How many layers, and on which surfaces?** Paint only, or wheels and interior too?
- **What does the warranty cover, and on what conditions?** Ask about annual inspections, required reapplication and any cap on payments.
- **Is the price fixed?** And if not, what would change it?
