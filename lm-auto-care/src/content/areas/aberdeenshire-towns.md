Past the edge of the city, cars work differently. They do longer runs, on smaller roads, through more weather. They carry dogs to beaches and boots to hills. Some of them have been up a farm track this morning. If you live in an Aberdeenshire town or village and you're fed up driving into Aberdeen to queue at a car wash, we can come to you.

## Towns we cover

Our area is Aberdeen and the surrounding areas. Out in the shire, that includes:

- **Ellon**, up the A90 to the north
- **Balmedie**, between the city and Ellon, with the beach and the dunes
- **Newmachar**, north-west of Dyce
- **Inverurie**, up the A96
- **Banchory**, out along Deeside
- **Stonehaven**, down the coast to the south

Somewhere in between? Send us your postcode. Plenty of villages near these towns are fine; we just don't want to list every one and promise something we'd have to check.

## What the shire does to a car

**Farm tracks and back roads.** Even if you don't farm, you'll drive behind someone who does. Mud on the road after wet weather, tractors pulling out of fields, gravel at the end of a single-track road. All of it ends up in the wheel arches, along the sills and on the back of the car. A 4x4 that's been up a track can come home caked to the door handles.

**Gritted B-roads.** In winter the main roads get gritted. The smaller roads get gritted when there's time, which means more slush, more spray and a mix of salt and mud on the car through the colder months. It sits low on the bodywork for weeks if nobody gets it off.

**Beach dogs at Balmedie.** Balmedie beach is one of the best places in the north-east to take a dog. It's also the reason a lot of shire cars have sand in the boot carpet and wet dog hair on the back seat from about March to October. Sand gets into every seam. Wet dog hair grips the fabric harder than dry. [Pet Hair Removal](/pet-hair-removal-aberdeen/) is a £10 extra and covers the seats, carpets and boot.

**Long commutes.** Ellon to the city, Inverurie on the A96, Stonehaven up the A90. Those are a lot of motorway-speed miles a week, and everything thrown up by the traffic ahead lands on the front of your car. Bugs in the summer, salt in the winter, a fine road film all year round.

**Bigger cars.** Out of town there are more SUVs, 4x4s and 7-seaters. The supplement for those is fixed: **+£10 for large vehicles, SUVs and 4x4s, +£15 for 7-seaters and large MPVs.** It's printed on the price list, it's in the [calculator](/prices/), and it's not something we work out at the door.

## Which service suits the shire

**For the dog car:** the [Interior Valet](/interior-valet-aberdeen/) at £35, plus Pet Hair Removal at £10. On a large estate or SUV, add the £10 supplement. That's £55 to get the beach out of the boot.

**For the farm-track 4x4:** the [Exterior Valet](/exterior-valet-aberdeen/). Pre-wash and snow foam first, which matters more on a car this dirty, because the mud needs loosening before anything touches the paint.

**For a full reset:** the [Full Works](/full-valet-aberdeen/), our full valet. Inside and out, wax protection, seats and carpets cleaned, and a final quality check before we sign it off. A good one to book in spring after a long winter on the back roads.

## What we need

**A water supply and a power socket where the car is.** Most houses in the towns and villages have an outside tap and a socket in reach, so it's usually easy. If it isn't, tell us, or ask about drop-off.

We book weekday mornings or afternoons, and weekend evenings.
