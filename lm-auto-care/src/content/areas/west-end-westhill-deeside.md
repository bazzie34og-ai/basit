The west side of Aberdeen is two different places with the same problem. In the city end, Rosemount, the West End, Mannofield, it's tight streets, tenement flats and cars parked nose to tail under the trees. Further out along the Dee it's Cults and Bieldside, Milltimber and Peterculter, then Westhill on its own up the hill: bigger driveways, bigger cars, and a lot of muddy dog walks.

Both ends need their cars cleaned. Neither wants to queue at a car wash to do it. That's where a mobile valeting service makes sense: we come to the car.

## Where we cover in the west

**City side:** Rosemount, Old Aberdeen, Mannofield, Garthdee and Hazlehead.

**Deeside:** Cults, Bieldside, Milltimber and Peterculter.

**Out west:** Westhill.

If your street's not on the list, message us your postcode. A lot of places in between are covered without us listing every one.

## What the west does to a car

**Trees.** The West End and Deeside are leafy, which is lovely until you park under them. Sap drips onto the paint and goes tacky. Leaves get into the scuttle panel under the windscreen and the channels round the boot. In autumn they pile up in the wheel arches and stay wet. Bird mess, from the same trees, is acidic and shouldn't sit on paint for long. An [Exterior Valet](/exterior-valet-aberdeen/) with snow foam loosens all of that before a hand touches the paint.

**Dog walks.** The Deeside Way runs right through this part of the world, along the old railway line. Hazlehead woods are on the doorstep for the West End. Countesswells, too. They all end the same: a wet, muddy dog in the boot, paw prints on the back seat, hair woven into the carpet. [Pet Hair Removal](/pet-hair-removal-aberdeen/) is a £10 extra and covers the seats, the carpets and the boot. Pair it with the [Interior Valet](/interior-valet-aberdeen/) and it's £45 for a small car.

**Country roads.** Head out past Peterculter or up towards Westhill and you're on narrower roads with farm traffic, mud on the tarmac after wet weather, and gritting that's patchier than on the main routes. The lower half of the car takes the hit. A proper wash that gets into the arches and along the sills is worth doing more often in winter.

**Tenement parking.** In Rosemount and the West End, the car lives on the street. It gets everything the street gets: dust, spray from passing traffic, leaves, the odd seagull. And there's nowhere to wash it. That's a problem we can sometimes solve, as long as there's water and power we can reach.

## Which service suits the west

**For dog owners:** the [Interior Valet](/interior-valet-aberdeen/) plus Pet Hair Removal. This is the most common booking we see from the Deeside side.

**For the car parked under the trees:** the [Exterior Valet](/exterior-valet-aberdeen/) at £35, every few weeks in autumn when the sap and leaves are at their worst.

**For the bigger family car:** the [Full Works](/full-valet-aberdeen/) once or twice a year, and the Basic Clean in between. There are a lot of large SUVs and estates round Westhill and Cults, so remember the size supplement: +£10 for large vehicles and SUVs, +£15 for 7-seaters. The [price calculator](/prices/) shows your total before you book.

## A note on flats

If you live in a flat in Rosemount or the West End, the honest answer is that water and power can be tricky. We need **a water supply and a power socket where the car is**. Some flats have a shared outside tap or a garage with a socket. Some don't. Message us before booking and we'll talk it through. If it doesn't work at the flat, ask about drop-off.
