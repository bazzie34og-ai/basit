North Aberdeen is where a lot of the city's cars spend their working day. The industrial estates round Dyce and Kirkhill, the airport and the heliport, the business parks off the A96, and the long commute in and out on the A90. If you live or work up this end, your car does a lot of miles in a lot of weather, and most of those miles are on busy dual carriageway.

We come to you. Your driveway in Bridge of Don, the car park at work in Dyce, the street outside your flat in Northfield. No queue, no drive-through, no hour lost at the weekend.

## What north Aberdeen does to a car

**The A90 commute.** The road north from the Bridge of Don to Ellon, and the AWPR loop round the back of the city, carry a lot of traffic at speed. That means spray. In wet weather every car picks up a fine grey film from the vehicles in front, and from November it's salty. It sits on the lower panels, the back of the car and the wheels. A regular [Exterior Valet](/exterior-valet-aberdeen/) gets it off before it's had weeks to settle: pre-wash and snow foam to loosen it, then a hand wash, a microfibre dry and a wax spray to help the next lot slide off.

**Industrial estate dust.** Park all day near the yards and depots round Dyce and Bucksburn and you'll know about it. There's dust from lorries and building sites, and the wheels pick up whatever's on the road. Brake dust plus estate grime is a stubborn mix on alloys.

**The airport car parks.** Leave a car at the airport for a week or two and it comes back with bird mess, tree sap if it was under the trees, and a film of exhaust from everything around it. A lot of people in the north of the city work away, offshore or otherwise, and come home to a car that's been sat. That's partly why we wrote a page for [offshore workers](/offshore-workers/).

**New-build life.** Bridge of Don and Kingswells have a lot of newer family estates. Family cars get the kids' crumbs down the side of the car seat, mud from the football on a Saturday, juice in the cup holders. The [Basic Clean](/mini-valet-aberdeen/), our mini valet at £30, keeps on top of that if it's booked every few weeks. A Baby Seat Clean is a £10 extra if the child seat needs its own attention.

**Wind off the sea.** Bridge of Don is close enough to the coast to get the salt air and the wind that comes with it. Cars parked facing the sea pick up a salty bloom on the glass.

## Which service suits the north

Most of our north Aberdeen bookings fall into three groups:

1. **Commuters** who want the outside sorted every few weeks. The [Exterior Valet](/exterior-valet-aberdeen/) at £35, or the [mobile car wash](/mobile-car-wash-aberdeen/) from £10 for a quicker job.
2. **Families** who want the inside kept in order. The [Basic Clean](/mini-valet-aberdeen/) or the [Interior Valet](/interior-valet-aberdeen/).
3. **People home from working away** who want the car reset. The Thorough Clean at £60, or the [Full Works](/full-valet-aberdeen/) at £85.

Bigger cars carry the same fixed supplement wherever you are: +£5 for medium, +£10 for large and SUVs, +£15 for 7-seaters. There's a lot of SUVs and 7-seaters on the estates up here, so it's worth checking your total on the [price calculator](/prices/) before you message.

## What we need from you

The same as everywhere: **a water supply and a power socket where the car is.** On a driveway with an outside tap, that's easy. At work, check with your employer that it's OK for us to use them and to clean the car on site. If neither works, drop-off is available, so ask us about it when you book.

Booking times are weekday mornings or afternoons, and weekend evenings.
