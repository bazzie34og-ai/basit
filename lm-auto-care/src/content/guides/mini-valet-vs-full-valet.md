Every week someone asks us a version of the same question: *do I need the full one, or will the mini valet do?* It's a fair question. The full valet costs nearly three times as much, and nobody wants to pay for work the car doesn't need.

So here's the honest version. No upselling. Just what each one does, what it doesn't, and how to tell which one your car is due.

## What a mini valet is for

A mini valet is maintenance. It's the car equivalent of hoovering the living room: it doesn't change anything dramatically, but it stops things building up.

Ours is called the **Basic Clean**, and it's £30 for a small car. It covers:

- an exterior wash
- wheels cleaned
- an interior vacuum
- the dashboard and surfaces wiped
- windows cleaned
- a quick finishing touch

That's it, and that's the point. It's quick, it gets the grit off the paint before it sits there for another fortnight, and it gets the crumbs out of the footwell before they're trodden into the carpet. On their own price list it's described as "perfect for keeping your car fresh between full cleans", which is exactly right.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't shampoo the seats, it doesn't clean the carpets beyond a hoover, and it doesn't include snow foam or wax. If those things need doing, a mini valet isn't the tool.

## What a full valet is for

A full valet resets the car. Ours is the **Full Works**, £85 for a small car, and it's the complete list:

- thorough exterior wash, with pre-wash and snow foam first
- detailed wheel clean
- thorough hand wash
- wax spray protection and tyre dressing
- full interior vacuum
- seats and carpets cleaned
- dashboard and trims cleaned
- door cards and centre console
- interior windows
- detailed finishing touches
- and a final quality check before it's signed off

The difference isn't just more items. It's depth. The mini valet hoovers the carpet; the full valet cleans it. The mini valet wipes the dash; the full valet does the trims, the door cards, the console, the inside of the glass. The mini valet washes the car; the full valet pre-washes and foams it first, so the grit's loosened and rinsed before anything rubs against the paint, then protects it with wax afterwards.

## The middle option people forget

Between the two there's the **Thorough Clean** at £60. It's our £35 Interior Valet and our £35 Exterior Valet together: a thorough clean inside and out to get the car looking fresh, clean and presentable again.

If the car needs more than a tidy but you don't need the full detail, this is often the right call. It's also what most people book before [selling a car or handing back a lease](/valet-before-selling-or-lease-return/), unless it's going in an advert with photos, when the Full Works is worth the extra.

## How to tell which one your car needs

A few honest rules of thumb.

### Book the mini valet if…

- you had a proper clean in the last couple of months
- the inside is messy rather than dirty: wrappers, crumbs, a bit of dust
- you mainly want the outside washed and the inside hoovered
- you're booking regularly, every few weeks, to stay on top of it

### Book the full valet if…

- it's been six months or more since anyone did the inside properly
- the carpets are stained or the seats have marks, not just crumbs
- it's the end of winter and there's salt and grit in every arch and footwell
- you've bought a used car and want it to feel like yours
- you're selling it, or photographing it for an advert

### Book something else if…

- **it's mostly dog hair.** Add [Pet Hair Removal](/pet-hair-removal-aberdeen/) for £10. A full valet without it will still leave hair woven into the boot carpet.
- **it's only the outside.** The Exterior Valet at £35 gets you snow foam, a hand wash and wax without paying for an interior you're happy with.
- **it's only the inside.** The Interior Valet, also £35, does seats, carpets, door cards and the inside windows.

## How often should you valet your car?

There's no single answer, but here's what works for most cars in Aberdeen.

**A daily car with no dogs or kids:** a mini valet every four to six weeks, a full valet in spring and autumn.

**A family car:** a mini valet every three or four weeks, a full valet two or three times a year, and a Baby Seat Clean (£10) every so often because nobody wants to know what's down the side of that seat.

**A car that does lots of winter miles:** more washing between November and March than in summer. Road salt sits in the arches and on the sills, and a regular exterior wash keeps it moving. There's more on that in our note on [road salt and grit through an Aberdeen winter](/guides/winter-road-salt-car-care-aberdeen/).

**A car with a dog in the boot:** depends entirely on the dog.

If you're asking the question, the car probably needs more than it's had. If it's been cleaned properly recently, the mini valet keeps it there. If it hasn't, start with a Thorough Clean or the Full Works, then keep it there with a Basic Clean every few weeks.
