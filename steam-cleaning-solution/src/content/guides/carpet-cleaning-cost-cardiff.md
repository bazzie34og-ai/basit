## What changes the price

### Size

The obvious one. A box room costs less than a through lounge. Stairs are priced by the flight, and a landing is usually added on.

### The condition of the carpet

A carpet that’s cleaned every year or two takes less time than one that hasn’t been cleaned in ten. Heavy soiling, deep traffic lanes and lots of stains mean more pre-treatment and more passes with the wand.

### Stains and odours

Pet accidents need a specific treatment that reaches the underlay, which is why pet odour treatment is often priced per room on top of a standard clean. Fresh coffee spills are quick. Old red wine takes longer.

### The type of carpet

Wool and deep-pile carpets take more care, and usually more time, than short-pile synthetic carpets. Rugs vary hugely depending on what they’re made of.

### How many rooms you book together

A whole house is usually better value per room than a single room on its own, because setting up takes the same time either way. It’s worth asking about stairs, landing and hallway together.

### Access and parking

A space by the front door makes the job quicker. A third-floor flat with no lift and parking two streets away takes longer.

## Sofas, stairs and the rest

Carpets are only part of the picture. The same price guides put a sofa clean at roughly £120 to £200 in Cardiff, and stairs and a landing at £60 to £120. Here’s what moves those numbers.

- **Sofas** are priced on size and fabric. A two-seater takes less time than a corner sofa with loose cushions and a chaise. Delicate fabrics need more care and a patch test first.
- **Stairs** are cleaned step by step with a hand tool, so a long or winding staircase takes longer than a short straight one. Many people book the stairs with the landing and hallway, because those carpets wear together.
- **Mattresses** are priced by size, and by whether there are stains or accidents to treat.
- **Rugs** depend on size, fibre and thickness. A thick wool rug takes far longer than a thin synthetic one.
- **End of tenancy** jobs are often priced for the whole property, so you get one figure for everything on the check-out list.

If you’re booking more than one thing, send photos of everything together. It’s usually cheaper than booking each job separately, and you only need to be in once.

## What a fair quote should include

When you compare quotes, check whether they include:

- **pre-treatment of stains**, or only the main clean
- **moving furniture**, and putting it back
- **pet odour treatment**, if you need it
- **drying equipment**, such as air movers
- **any call-out or travel charge**
- **VAT**, if the company is VAT-registered

Some low headline prices are only the starting point, with extras added on the day. A fixed price, agreed before anyone arrives, avoids that.

## Watch out for

- **Very low prices per room** that turn into much higher bills once “extras” are added at the door.
- **Quotes given over the phone with no photo**, then “revised” once someone sees the carpet.
- **Pressure to add sofa or protector treatments** you didn’t ask for.

In reviews of carpet cleaners around Cardiff, the thing people praise most is simple: the price they were quoted was the price they paid, with no upselling. That’s what you should expect.

## Is it worth paying for a professional clean?

A professional clean costs more than hiring a machine for the day, but it usually gets much more dirt out and leaves much less water behind. That means a carpet that stays clean for longer and dries the same day. For a deposit, a house sale or a carpet you want to last, it’s usually worth it.

For a small, fresh spill, though, careful blotting and a little cool water may be all you need. We’re happy to tell you that if you ask.
